# Camera evidence
Differentiate Camera, Stream, Observation, TrackLocal, TrackGlobal and Person. A track is not civil identity. Read camera calibration, coverage, observation timestamp, confidence, association evidence and missing-data intervals. Do not claim a person was absent because a camera failed to detect them.
