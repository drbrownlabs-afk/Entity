The models were imported with Blender's official glTF importer. Scene instances are positioned/scaled for this estimated reconstruction; dining chairs are fitted on three axes to keep the existing footprint. Velvet/pouf finishes are recolored, plant animation and fireflies are omitted, and selected mapped foliage is arranged into the vertical garden and hanging planters. These are derivative scene instances; the downloaded assets are not overwritten.

The HDRI supplies generic urban reflections/background. It does not identify the actual location or certify sun orientation. The Blender clock controls sun/sky and context brightness; no actual sensor, business event or geographical observation is generated.

New terrazzo/rug textures, screen graphics, artwork, fixtures and workstation details are authored in the project. Operator JPGs in `docs/spatial-simulation/references/` are quality/design references only, and are never substituted for rendered evidence.

For later PlayCanvas work, retain this attribution with distributed derivatives. Validate support for sheen/specular, transmission, texture transforms and WebP, and compare actual runtime rendering with the Blender review. No architectural export or PlayCanvas implementation is performed in this revision.
